Frequently asked questions

The basics of using your account.

How do I add funds?

Use the Credit or Add Fund button on your dashboard. You'll be taken to the payment gateway to pay, and your balance is credited once the payment is confirmed.

How does staking work?

Choose an amount and a lock period (14, 21 or 28 days). Your funds are locked for that period. Staking more adds to your stake and shifts the unlock date to the newest period.

When can I unstake or claim my reward?

After the lock period completes. Before that, Unstake and Claim are not available — you would need to use Emergency Withdraw to exit early, which carries a penalty.

What is Emergency Withdraw?

It lets you exit a locked stake early. You receive part of your staked amount back and forfeit the rest and any pending reward. The exact figures are shown before you confirm.

How do I withdraw?

Use the Withdraw box, choose a network, and paste your wallet address. Your balance is debited and the payout is processed once approved.

Need more help?

Open the Support page from the menu and send us a message.
